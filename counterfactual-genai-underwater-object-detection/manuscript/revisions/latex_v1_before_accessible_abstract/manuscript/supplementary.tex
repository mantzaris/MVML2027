\section{Controlled Representation Study}

This companion study separates exact construction labels from unknown real-world audibility. Fully synthetic scenes comprise colored ambient noise and zero, one or two harmonic, amplitude-modulated or chirped sources. Received supports include bounded multipath delay. Overlapping source supports are merged into the union of activity for temporal scoring. A union-activity detector does not resolve overlapping physical objects. The separately trained temporal spectral CNN supplies the fully/semi-synthetic results; these do not test Qwen/flow benefit.\par

The focused representation study uses three-second controlled clips and fixed detection heads for STFT, STFT plus wavelets, STFT plus bounded HHT, and all views. It separates training/development/test generation identities and a held-out signal family. Presence AP in this study is not temporal AP and does not directly establish a reasoning-agent benefit. HHT remains an explicitly exploratory approximation.\par

Temporal AP uses greedy confidence-ordered one-to-one matching with the monotone precision envelope. The unconstrained development replay had 50.0\% valid actions across 4,220 actions from 2,110 clip-seed records (including partial runs). It failed by proposing already observed STFT after wavelet acquisition. This engineering failure and its traces are preserved. Before test evaluation, the controller was amended to finite legal-JSON token-trie decoding, enforcing schema validity by construction and retaining the independent parser. EOS is required, observed tools are excluded, and no retries choose a favorable action. The 160-state GPU fixture checks integration, not reasoning competence. All 3,600 test clip-seed paths were wavelet, Fourier, then verify; generation informed no later action and did not demonstrate adaptive selection.\par

Experiment recovery uses project-local atomic checkpoints, checksummed generation metadata, a hash-chained ledger and verified prediction shards. Restarting recomputes unfinished work and preserves completed outputs. Source/configuration/data fingerprints guard against accidental resume under a different experiment.\par

All code, data, caches, models, figures, reports and restart artifacts remain inside the new MVML2027 submission directory. The earlier submission is unchanged. Large raw data and checkpoints remain outside version control; no publication or submission is performed by generating these drafts.\par

The main limitations remain five collection days in one dependence component, AIS-derived labels, one port/deployment, 600/600 artificial test balance, a fixed generator seed and construction-only temporal annotation. AP/precision and calibration are not deployment-prevalence estimates. The supplement reports failed numerical assumptions alongside successful fixtures.\par
