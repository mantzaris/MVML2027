# MVML 2027 submission requirements audit

Checked 23 November 2026 against the official public pages and the downloaded paper template. This is a submission-preparation note, not confirmation that a manuscript has been submitted or accepted.

## Verified public requirements

The [author guidelines](https://mvml.org/author-guidelines/) cap full papers at eight pages and short papers at four. They require the appropriate template, English text and electronic submission; LaTeX is also accepted. No reference-page exception appears, so keep references inside the eight-page total. No public anonymity requirement or separate supplemental allowance was found on these guidelines, the ethics page, or the visible [ACSS login](https://mvml.org/acss/login). The authenticated submission fields were not inspected. Anonymous authors in the current draft are placeholders, not an asserted conference rule. A separate 3–4 page study remains a companion draft until the portal or organizers establish how it may accompany the submission.

The [dates page](https://mvml.org/important-dates/) lists submission on 21 January 2027, notification on 25 February, final accepted manuscripts on 18 March, and the conference on 15–17 August. Recheck before submission.

The [ethics policy](https://mvml.org/ethics/) requires accurate and reproducible reporting, proper attribution, genuine consenting authors, and prohibits simultaneous submission of the same manuscript to multiple venues. No conference-specific generative-AI disclosure instruction was located on the inspected pages; this absence is not evidence about unpublished portal requirements.

## Template inspection

The [official DOCX](https://mvml.org/documents/MVML27_Paper_Template.docx) is retained unchanged at `protocol/conference_sources/MVML27_Paper_Template.docx`, SHA256 `86364438af0f7090bc905442437f88c8f72412770d040e885415402eb2c19a35`. It was rendered with bundled LibreOffice and all three pages visually inspected. The written instructions specify Letter paper, 25 mm margins, single spacing, 7 mm first-line indent, Times New Roman 11-point body, Arial 16-point bold title and 12/11-point bold headings. The abstract is one paragraph of at most 300 words without citations; use 4–8 keywords and IEEE references. Figure text should preferably be at least 10 points. Figures and tables need body citations, numbering and explanatory captions. Names, affiliations and email placeholders are present. Assigned proceedings codes belong to the accepted final version.

The file contains internal inconsistencies: its actual XML left/right margins are 680 twips (about 12 mm), while the written instructions say 25 mm; its default Normal style is 12 points, while written body instructions say 11. Preserve the file and distinguish written guidance from incidental XML. The report builder follows the written 25 mm/11-point choices but uses Tinos/Arimo substitutes and a draft footer. It should not be described as an exact reproduction of the official template. Final author metadata, proceedings fields, figure readability and template conformity need a submission-stage pass; they do not block research evaluation.
