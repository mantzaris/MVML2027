Anonymous manuscript source package

Compile with XeLaTeX (not pdfLaTeX). Required fonts are Tinos and Arimo.
Required TeX packages include fontspec, newtxmath, amsmath, amssymb,
graphicx, booktabs, array, tabularx, float, placeins, titlesec, caption,
enumitem, geometry and hyperref.

From the extracted directory, run:

  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -no-shell-escape main.tex
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -no-shell-escape main.tex

Compile supplement.tex separately using the same command twice.
No BibTeX step is needed. References are included in main.tex.
Keep the figures directory alongside the TeX files.

main.pdf is the eight-page paper with four figures.
supplement.pdf is the four-page companion with four additional figures.
The main paper can be read independently of the supplement.

The .md files provide editable narrative copies. The .tex files are the
complete buildable sources. Editing Markdown alone will not update TeX.
No datasets, training checkpoints or experiment logs are needed to build.

Tinos and Arimo are metric-compatible alternatives to Times New Roman
and Arial. Confirm the final conference formatting and upload requirements
before submission, including anonymity and any supplemental upload.
