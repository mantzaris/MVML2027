Operator-oriented reading guide to the saved UniqueShip civilian-recording study. Left: the shared classifier correctly labels 1,119 of 2,240 test clips (50.0%). Category bars show recall, with exact counts and the most frequent wrong label; confusion percentages use all clips in the relevant category. Right: three calibrated reporting systems evaluated on the same 100 clips, requesting four physical details per clip. Supported and wrong/unsupported assertions are counts across all 400 requested slots. Uncertain slots and withheld reports contribute no supported assertion. Whiskers rescale the existing source-bootstrap 95% intervals to 400 requests. Retained subsets and tool choices differ between systems. Learned selection uses 99 fewer tool calls, returns 16 fewer supported details, and makes 8 fewer wrong/unsupported assertions than the locked heuristic. This is a completeness/cost/error trade-off, not an improvement in category accuracy. The fixed template supplies 90 more supported details and 15 more wrong/unsupported details than the heuristic at its own calibrated operating point. Support is an automated proxy; no human/operator study or operational sonar validation was performed. No new experiments or test tuning were used for this figure.
