# Prospective identification follow-up

The user requested a substantive refactor toward identifying acoustic objects after reviewing the original negative evidence-acquisition results. Here identification means the dataset's five civilian source categories (background, cargo, passenger, tanker, tug/tow), not individual hull identity, localization, submarine recognition or validated operational sonar performance. The original study, its frozen code, outcomes and failed success criterion remain intact. This follow-up is explicitly motivated by those observed results.

## Question and proposed method

Can source-balanced exemplar evidence and measured spectral/modulation features improve vessel-category identification over a frozen acoustic classifier and a matched acoustic-only refit? Can disagreement between those evidence sources identify recordings that should be referred for review?

The proposed identification system combines a frozen Swin acoustic representation with (1) class-conditional similarity to distinct training-source prototypes and (2) numerical spectrum, envelope and waveform-quality features. A regularized multinomial logistic model is fit on the original policy-fit partition. Each class and each source within class receive equal total fit weight. The representation, prototype bank and PCA are learned only from original acoustic-fit data. Probability temperature is fitted on development. A separate, development-selected disagreement penalty ranks review priority; the final threshold is fixed on calibration to target 70% automatic reporting. Measurements support their own acoustic facts, not a physical proof of a vessel class. Generative wording has no authority to change the classifier's identification.

Fusion, retrieval and selective classification already exist. The contribution being tested is this specific source-balanced identification/referral design, its controlled ablations and a fresh real-recording evaluation, not a claim to have invented those ingredients. A negative result remains a valid outcome and is not rebranded as improved identification.

## Data boundaries

Training/selection/calibration use the original non-test partitions. The original test results motivate the follow-up but neither its individual labels nor predictions are used to fit parameters, choose settings or select examples. New test candidates must (a) be absent from the original 7,418 records, (b) have a source group absent from **every** original partition, (c) have UTC dates absent from all original non-test partitions, and (d) be at least 65 seconds from all original non-test recording starts. A deterministic source-first sample requests 50 clips/class, at most three/source and at least ten minutes apart. Any shortfall is retained. All downloaded members are CRC checked; decoded hashes are compared with original data and across the new cohort.

The new cohort is a custom held-out extension from previously excluded members of the same release; it is **not the official fold's test set**. Its dates can overlap the previous study's test dates. Therefore it tests fresh recorded sources in the same collection, not a new deployment or ocean. Original parent-recording and deployment metadata remain unavailable. AI review cannot supply independent human identification ground truth; class scoring uses the released dataset labels, which themselves derive from the dataset's acquisition/labeling process.

## Comparisons, selection and freeze

All identification methods predict all five classes. Baselines are the original seed-17 classifier, the three-seed probability ensemble, a source-balanced acoustic-only refit on the same policy-fit labels, a physical-feature-only classifier and class-prototype voting. Ablations remove numerical features, remove prototype evidence, or use the full fused model's maximum probability without the disagreement penalty. All learned refits share the same regularization grid and development-only macro-F1 selection. The primary comparator is locked as the development-best of anchor, ensemble and acoustic-only refit. The proposed full fusion is retained even if an ablation performs better; no test-based model replacement is allowed.

Before any fresh test inference, save a timestamped freeze of this protocol, configuration, exact source files, selected models, normalization and prototype parameters, thresholds, cohort IDs and raw-audio integrity audit. This is an internal prospective record, not external preregistration. Save the development search table, including unsuccessful settings. Predictions and detailed logs are excluded from Git.

## Endpoints and statistics

Primary: five-class macro-F1 on every fresh clean clip, with a paired source-cluster percentile bootstrap (2,000 resamples) comparing full fusion to the development-locked comparator. Success requires a gain of at least 0.03 and a positive 95% lower bound. Also report accuracy, confusion matrices, per-class recall and vessel/background balanced accuracy. The latter pools vessel-category probabilities and uses a development-selected binary threshold; it is classification of provided clips, not continuous event detection.

At calibration-locked reporting thresholds, report correct labels, wrong labels and referrals per 100 requested clips, actual coverage and accuracy among issued labels. Rejection receives no credit as a correct identification. Curves at exact test-ranked coverage are descriptive comparisons, not deployable thresholds or statistical coverage guarantees. Show the maximum-probability and disagreement policies on the same full-fusion predictions so the review-ranking contribution is isolated.

Fresh waveform stress tests use 20/10/0 dB Gaussian noise, a 2 kHz low-pass, gain 0.25 and digital clipping on the same fresh source clips. Models, temperatures and reporting thresholds remain fixed. Report every condition without selecting only favorable ones. These artificial perturbations retain nominal dataset category labels but do not establish perceptual recognizability or real deployment prevalence. A fixed cyclic permutation of prototype class evidence tests dependence on that evidence. A UTC-day-cluster bootstrap is a dependence sensitivity. Per-class/ablation/stress comparisons are exploratory and do not expand the single primary success test.

The operator-facing output will show the provisional category, category probabilities, referral status and numerical evidence separately. Report volume is a proxy for review quantity. No human time savings, workload reduction, operator accuracy, contact continuity, false alerts/hour or individual vessel identification is measured.

Three frozen Omni classification comparators use the fresh clean cohort: audio alone, audio plus the original acoustic posterior, and audio plus the fused posterior and compact measured evidence. They share a new category-only prompt, greedy 128-token decoding and one JSON object containing a category (or uncertainty) and one short reason. A development-only format pilot (up to four clips/class) must reach at least 95% valid outputs before freezing. Truncation and malformed output count as failed/withheld identifications; they are never dropped or regenerated. Generator methods are supplementary and never replace the development-locked primary comparator. Compare generator labels with the numeric classifier to count correct labels changed to errors and errors corrected. The full fusion classifier's label remains authoritative in the proposed operator-facing record; a free-form generator's apparent agreement is not evidence of better category accuracy.
